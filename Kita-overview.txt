# 📄 Kita — AI-Powered Smart POS & Financial Intelligence System (Final Overview with KYC)

---

## 1. Project Title

**Kita — AI-Powered Smart POS, Financial Intelligence, and Credit Readiness System**

---

## 2. Executive Summary

Kita is a mobile-first, AI-powered **Point-of-Sale (POS) and financial intelligence platform** designed for *karenderya*, sari-sari stores, and small Filipino businesses.

The system automates daily sales, digitizes expenses through **OCR receipt scanning**, and generates real-time financial insights. These data points are transformed into a **Loan Readiness Report**, enabling small business owners to prove their financial capacity and qualify for microfinance loans.

To ensure credibility and trust, Kita integrates **KYC (Know Your Customer) verification**, confirming that users are legitimate business owners. This strengthens the reliability of financial data and increases acceptance by microfinance institutions such as RAFI.

> **Kita converts real business activity into verified financial identity and loan eligibility.**

---

## 3. Problem Statement

### 3.1 Operational Challenges

Small business owners:

* Manually record sales and expenses
* Lack real-time inventory tracking
* Do not know actual profit
* Struggle to manage receipts

### 3.2 Financial Challenges

* No formal financial records
* Cannot prove income or business legitimacy
* Low access to loans and microfinance

### 3.3 Trust & Verification Gap

* Lenders cannot verify:

  * If the business is real
  * If the data is reliable
* Leads to:

  * Loan rejection
  * Higher perceived risk

> Result: Businesses remain **invisible and untrusted by financial institutions**

---

## 4. Proposed Solution

Kita provides an integrated system combining:

* 📱 Smart POS for real-time sales tracking
* 📷 OCR-based receipt scanning
* 📊 Automated analytics and profit tracking
* 🤖 AI-powered financial insights
* 📄 Loan Readiness Report generation
* 🪪 **KYC Verification for business owners** ⭐

---

## 5. Core System Workflow

1. **KYC Verification (Onboarding)**

   * User verifies identity and business legitimacy

2. **Record Sales**

   * Transactions logged via POS

3. **Scan Expenses**

   * Receipts processed via OCR

4. **Track Inventory**

   * Real-time stock updates

5. **Analyze Data**

   * AI generates insights and trends

6. **Generate Loan Readiness Report**

   * Combines verified identity + financial data

---

### 6.7 🪪 KYC Verification Module ⭐ NEW

Ensures users are **legitimate business owners**.

#### Features:

* 📸 ID Verification

  * Upload government-issued ID
* 🤳 Selfie Verification

  * Face match with ID
* 🏪 Business Validation

  * Store photo (e.g., karenderya setup)
  * Optional barangay/business permit upload
* 📍 Basic Information

  * Name, location, type of business

#### Output:

* Verified status badge (e.g., ✅ Verified Business Owner)

* Increases chances of loan approval

### For Microfinance Institutions:

* Confirms borrower identity
* Reduces fraud risk
* Improves trust in submitted data

> Combined with financial data, KYC creates a **complete borrower profile**
